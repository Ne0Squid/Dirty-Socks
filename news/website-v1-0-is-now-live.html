Dirty Socks 2.0 is now live

Website 2.0 is live! Here is everything that has changed since the relaunch on November 26: a new look, a faster site, Battle.net login with raid sign-ups, a character page for every guild member, and this News section. Read on for the full list, and if you spot anything that looks off, tell an officer in Discord and we will get it fixed.

New look

  • New navigation. The Dirty Socks logo sits in the middle of the bar next to Home, Roster, Hall of Fame and Recruitment. The Horde / Alliance switch now lives in the bar, and the raid countdown sits under the buttons on the home page. Pre-Raid BiS, Dungeon Loot and Patch Notes moved to the footer, under Tools.
  • New logos and artwork. Horde and Alliance versions of the new DS logo, a big banner behind the home page intro, a new browser icon and a new link preview image when the site is shared in Discord.
  • Darker theme with deeper backgrounds and richer Horde and Alliance colors.
  • Wider pages on desktop, and a new three-column footer with our tagline: Socks ain't clean.
  • Better on phones. The menu, login and countdown all fit the small screen (the Horde / Alliance switch moves into the menu).

Home page

  • Race to 60 is now a podium. The winner gets a big card and everyone else is listed under Standings in finishing order.
  • Cleaner layout. The guild roster, professions and craft finder moved to the Roster page, and the old leaderboard and Guild Stats boxes are gone.
  • News slider right below the intro (see News below).

Roster

  • Roster at a Glance: how many tanks, healers, melee and ranged we have, plus guild averages.
  • Guild Roster now shows 20 members per page, with Professions and the Craft Finder right underneath.
  • Every name links to that player's character page.

Recruitment

  • The raid schedule, the raid roster and Request a Raid Spot now live on one Recruitment page. Raid roster tables start collapsed to keep the page short.
  • Sign up with Battle.net is the new way to ask for a spot. The Google form is still there if you would rather not sign in.
  • The raid roster fills itself from confirmed sign-ups, so it is always current.

Log in with Battle.net and raid sign-ups

  • Log in from the top left of any page. Once you are in, the bar shows your highest-level character, with a menu for My character, Raid sign-up and Sign out.
  • Raid sign-up: choose your character, then The Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20) or both. Your character details come straight from Battle.net, so what officers see is the real thing.
  • Officers approve or decline each raid separately and place approved players in a raid slot. You can leave one raid of a both-raids sign-up without losing the other.
  • New sign-ups are announced in our Discord.

Character pages

Every guild member now has an armory-style page:

  • Portrait, equipped gear with item tooltips, stats, professions with recipes, and dungeon and raid kills.
  • PvP panel beside the name and a Reputation card (top 5 shown, the rest under Show more factions).
  • Your own page also shows your sign-up status for both raids, and you can switch between your alts.
  • On phones and tablets, tap an item to see its tooltip.

News

  • Every post gets its own page at dirtysocks.net/news/ followed by the post title. Share the link with anyone.
  • The home page News slider shows the latest posts with a cover photo, the title and a short preview, plus a Read More button. Swipe, use the arrows or the dots, or let it scroll on its own.
  • Posts can have formatting and pictures.
  • All news lists every post in one place.

Behind the scenes

  • Our own address: the site now lives at dirtysocks.net, with clean page addresses such as /roster and /recruitment.
  • Faster: pages no longer load 3.7 MB of unused banner images.
  • Findable: a custom "page not found" screen, plus a sitemap so search engines can find us.
  • Item level is gone. Blizzard stopped sending item levels on November 24, so rather than show numbers that no longer update we removed them everywhere. The Best Geared and Guild Firsts awards are hidden until item levels come back.

Coming soon: Winter Veil

From December 1 to January 3 the site dresses up for the holidays: draped lights with swinging baubles, pine garlands, a snowy banner, Santa hats and a festive footer. Falling snow has an on/off button if you would rather skip it.

Thanks for being part of Dirty Socks.

Socks ain't clean, but the website is.
